About

I am a master's student at Nanjing University. My current research interests include large language model agents, agentic reinforcement learning, long-horizon reasoning, and AI for Science.

I am particularly interested in building autonomous agents that can reason, use tools, interact with environments, and improve their performance through learning and self-refinement.
